PYXIS

Five alphas, from a kernel that could start one terminal to a desktop with its own light, shape and motion. The screenshots were taken by booting each release's own disk image again, in November 2026.

α5November 2026

A desktop that behaves like light

Pyxis's own look and motion: the Daylight wallpaper, frosted waterdrop windows, Lumen motion, a lock screen, and every app in its own sandbox.

  • Cards and Alt+Tab, notifications and sound.
  • One password dialog for every root action.
